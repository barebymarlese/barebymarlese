Carbon Facial, Carbon Peel or Hollywood Peel?

These names are often used for the same style of laser facial. A fine layer of carbon lotion is applied to prepared skin, allowed to settle and then treated with controlled laser passes. The carbon helps the practitioner work across the skin in a structured way while the laser process is intended to support a cleaner and brighter-looking complexion.

Although “peel” appears in the popular name, this is not the same as applying a strong chemical peel. Suitability, expected sensations and aftercare are discussed before treatment.

Who may be suitable?

The treatment may be considered by adults seeking non-invasive skin rejuvenation with limited interruption to their routine. It can appeal to clients of different genders and ages, including those new to professional skin treatments.

It may be appropriate where the goal is cosmetic improvement rather than treatment of a diagnosed skin disease. Active conditions, medication, pregnancy, recent sun exposure, strong skincare and previous reactions can affect suitability, so an honest consultation is essential.

Consultation and patch testing

The consultation is part of the treatment pathway, not a quick formality. It protects your skin and supports realistic planning.

Medical and skin history

You will be asked about medication, allergies, previous reactions, skin conditions, recent procedures, pregnancy or breastfeeding, healing and any relevant medical history.

Products and lifestyle

Retinoids, exfoliating acids, benzoyl peroxide, prescription products, tanning, sun exposure and recent facials can influence treatment timing. Bring an accurate list of what you currently use.

Patch test and expectations

A patch test is completed where required. Your goals, likely response, downtime, aftercare and whether a single treatment or course makes sense are discussed before proceeding.

Treatment may be postponed if the skin is irritated, sunburnt, actively infected, recently treated with incompatible procedures, or if medication or products make it unsafe to proceed.

What happens on treatment day?

Appointments follow a clear process so you understand each stage and have time to ask questions.

Review and preparation

Your skin and any changes since consultation are reviewed. The area is cleansed and prepared, with eye protection used as appropriate.

Carbon application

A thin, even layer of carbon lotion is applied. It is allowed to settle before the laser stages begin.

Controlled laser passes

The laser is used in planned passes according to your skin and treatment response. Most people describe warmth and light snapping sensations.

Skin review

The skin is checked throughout. Settings are not increased simply to make the treatment feel stronger; the priority is controlled and appropriate treatment.

Finishing care

Remaining product is removed and suitable finishing products are applied where appropriate. Your immediate skin response is assessed.

Aftercare plan

You leave with practical advice covering cleansing, hydration, sun protection, makeup, heat, exercise and when to restart active skincare.

Results and realistic expectations

Some clients notice that skin looks cleaner and brighter soon after treatment, while others notice a smoother feel as temporary warmth or sensitivity settles. The degree and duration of improvement depend on baseline skin condition, oil production, home care, lifestyle and consistency.

A single appointment can suit a client wanting an occasional refresh. A course of three may be more appropriate where the goal is a planned series rather than a one-off treatment. Maintenance is always individual.

Plan ahead for important occasions. First-time clients should not book immediately before a wedding, regatta event, holiday or professional engagement because every skin response is individual.

Downtime and normal responses

Downtime is generally minimal, but “minimal” does not mean no response at all. Temporary warmth, mild redness, dryness, tightness or sensitivity can occur.

These effects often settle quickly, but recovery varies. Contact the clinic if you experience an unexpected or concerning reaction. Following aftercare and avoiding unnecessary irritation gives the skin the best opportunity to settle.

Carbon Facial before weddings and events

Allow enough time

Henley clients often enquire before weddings, parties, race days, holidays and photographs. A trial treatment well in advance is wiser than trying something new days before the event. This allows the consultation, patch test and your individual response to be understood.

Build a simple plan

Where a course is suitable, appointments can be planned around the event date rather than crowded together. Gentle home care, good sun protection and avoiding last-minute product changes are just as important as the treatment itself.

Aftercare

  • Use a gentle cleanser and avoid scrubbing
  • Keep skincare simple and hydrating
  • Avoid retinoids, exfoliating acids and benzoyl peroxide until advised
  • Avoid saunas, steam rooms, very hot showers and excessive heat initially
  • Delay intense exercise if the skin remains warm or sensitive
  • Use appropriate broad-spectrum sun protection
  • Avoid picking, rubbing or aggressive facial tools
  • Follow individual guidance on makeup

Carbon Facial and acne-prone skin

People often search for an “acne facial in Henley” when they mean a treatment for oily, congested or blemish-prone skin. A Carbon Facial may support the cosmetic appearance of oiliness and congestion in suitable clients, but it does not diagnose or cure acne.

Painful, inflamed, infected or medically complex skin should be assessed appropriately. Where necessary, you may be advised to seek medical or dermatology guidance before laser treatment.
