Risk Assessment

Home-based laser tattoo removal risk assessment for a dedicated treatment area. This document is intended to support safe working practice, council inspection readiness, insurer expectations, and regular review of hazards, controls, and further actions.

Assessment Details

Scope & People at Risk

Supporting Control Measures Checklist

  • Consultation, consent, and treatment records completed and stored securely.
  • Patch testing / suitability checks used where clinically appropriate.
  • Manufacturer instructions, training evidence, and service records retained.
  • Protective eyewear available, clean, and suitable for the equipment used.
  • Cleaning products and disinfectants used according to label and COSHH information.
  • Treatment room kept clean, ventilated, and free of avoidable trip hazards.
  • Business waste handled separately and disposed of through appropriate arrangements.
  • Incidents, near misses, and complaints reviewed to improve controls.
  • Assessment reviewed after incidents, equipment changes, layout changes, or updated guidance.
Important
This risk assessment should be reviewed whenever there is a change in equipment, treatment scope, products used, room layout, insurer requirements, local council conditions, or after any accident, near miss, or significant complaint.
