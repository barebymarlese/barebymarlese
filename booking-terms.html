Booking Terms

Please read these booking terms before arranging a consultation, patch test, or treatment appointment with BARE by Marlese.

1. Consultation & Patch Test

A consultation and patch test must be completed before laser treatment can begin. Treatment suitability is assessed during the consultation based on your treatment area, skin type, medical history, and treatment goals.

2. Consultation Deposit

A consultation deposit may be required to secure your appointment. Consultation deposits are non-refundable but may be transferred if at least 24 hours' notice is provided.

3. Treatment Packages

Bundle sessions must be paid in full at the time of purchase and are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.

Sessions not used within this period will expire and cannot be extended.

4. Appointment Changes & Cancellations

A minimum of 24 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule an appointment.

Late cancellations or missed appointments may result in the session being deducted from the bundle. Failure to attend an appointment without notice will result in the session being deducted.

5. Treatment Intervals

A minimum interval of 6 weeks between treatments is required to allow the skin to heal properly and for the body to gradually clear fragmented tattoo pigment.

6. Results & Expectations

Results vary depending on the treatment type, skin response, treatment area, lifestyle factors, and aftercare.

For tattoo removal, results are rarely linear. Whether your goal is complete removal or fading a tattoo for a cover-up, a personalised treatment plan will be created during your consultation.

No treatment outcome can be guaranteed.

7. Client Responsibility

Clients are responsible for providing accurate medical and treatment information, following aftercare advice, and attending appointments with suitable skin condition for treatment.

8. Payments

Payments are processed through secure third-party payment providers. BARE by Marlese does not store or have access to your full payment card or banking details.

9. Privacy

Your personal data, consultation information, images, and treatment records are handled in accordance with the BARE by Marlese Privacy Policy.

Important
By booking a consultation, patch test, or treatment appointment, you agree to these booking terms and understand that treatment suitability and outcomes vary between individuals.
