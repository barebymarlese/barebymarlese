Health & Safety Information

Professional Standards

  • Treatments are carried out by a VTCT Level 5 qualified practitioner.
  • All equipment is maintained, serviced, and used in accordance with manufacturer guidelines.
  • A full consultation and patch test are completed prior to treatment.
  • Treatment is only performed where it is safe and appropriate to do so.

Hygiene & Infection Control

  • All treatment areas are cleaned and disinfected before and after every client.
  • Single-use consumables are used wherever possible.
  • Hands are sanitised before and after every treatment.
  • Gloves and appropriate PPE are worn where required.
  • Cross-contamination prevention procedures are strictly followed.

Laser Safety

  • Laser treatments are performed in a controlled environment.
  • Protective eyewear is worn by both practitioner and client at all times.
  • Only appropriate settings are used based on skin type and treatment area.
  • Equipment is operated in line with safety protocols.

Treatment Risks

  • Redness, swelling, and sensitivity are common temporary effects.
  • Blistering, scabbing, or pigment changes may occur.
  • There is a small risk of scarring or infection.
  • Multiple sessions are required; complete removal cannot be guaranteed.

Client Responsibilities

  • Follow all aftercare instructions provided.
  • Avoid sun exposure and use SPF where required.
  • Inform the practitioner of any medical changes.
  • Report any unusual or severe reactions promptly.

Home Clinic Environment

  • Treatments are carried out in a clean, dedicated treatment space.
  • Appointments are managed to maintain a safe and controlled environment.
  • No more than limited clients are treated per week to ensure quality and safety.
Important
Laser tattoo removal carries risks. Individual results vary. By proceeding with treatment, clients acknowledge they have received consultation, understand the risks, and agree to follow all safety and aftercare guidance.
