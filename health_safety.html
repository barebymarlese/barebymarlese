Health & Safety Information

Professional Standards

  • Treatments are carried out by a VTCT Level 5 qualified practitioner.
  • All equipment is maintained and used according to manufacturer guidelines.
  • A consultation and patch test are completed prior to treatment.
  • Treatment is only performed where safe and appropriate.

Hygiene & Infection Control

  • All surfaces are disinfected between clients.
  • Single-use consumables are used where possible.
  • Hands are sanitised before and after treatments.
  • PPE is used where required.
  • Strict cross-contamination protocols are followed.

Laser Safety

  • Laser is operated in a controlled treatment environment.
  • Protective eyewear is worn by practitioner and client.
  • Settings are tailored to skin type and treatment area.
  • Equipment is used in line with safety standards.

Treatment Risks

  • Temporary redness, swelling, and sensitivity are common.
  • Blistering, pigmentation changes, or temporary skin irritation may occur.
  • There is a small risk of scarring or infection.
  • Multiple sessions may be required depending on the treatment and desired outcome.
  • Results vary between individuals and cannot be guaranteed.

Client Responsibilities

  • Follow all aftercare instructions.
  • Avoid sun exposure and use SPF.
  • Report any unusual reactions.
  • Inform practitioner of medical changes.

Home Clinic Environment

  • Treatment is performed in a dedicated clean space.
  • Appointments are controlled to maintain safety.
  • Low client volume ensures proper care and hygiene.
Important
Laser treatments carry risks including redness, swelling, pigmentation changes, blistering, scarring, and infection. Results vary between individuals and treatment types. By proceeding, clients acknowledge understanding of these risks and agree to follow all treatment and aftercare guidance.
