Carbon Facial, Carbon Peel or Hollywood Peel?

Clients use several names when searching for this treatment. The terminology can differ, but suitability and safe delivery matter more than the label.

Carbon Laser Facial

This describes the complete treatment: cleansing, carbon application and controlled laser passes selected for your skin and treatment plan.

Carbon Peel

The word “peel” refers to the fresh, polished appearance clients often seek. It is not the same as applying a traditional acid-based chemical peel.

Hollywood Peel

This popular name reflects the treatment’s reputation as a low-downtime skin refresh. Results vary, and every first appointment still requires consultation and patch testing.

Who may be suitable?

Suitability is assessed individually. The treatment may be considered for adults with stable, intact skin who are concerned about oiliness, congestion, dullness, visible pores or uneven-looking texture.

  • Clients wanting a professional skin refresh
  • People who prefer minimal downtime
  • Those willing to follow pre-treatment and aftercare advice

A consultation does not guarantee treatment. Safety and suitability come first.

When treatment may be postponed

Treatment may need to be delayed or declined when the skin barrier is compromised or when medical, product or lifestyle factors increase risk.

  • Active infection, cold sores, open skin or significant irritation
  • Recent tanning, sunburn or planned strong sun exposure
  • Use of certain photosensitising medication
  • Recent aggressive peels, laser procedures or microneedling
  • Recent use of strong retinoids or exfoliating products
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding or other contraindications identified during consultation

Consultation and patch test

The consultation is a clinical safety step, not a sales formality.

Medical and medication review

You will be asked about health conditions, medication, previous reactions, allergies and treatments that could affect how your skin responds to laser.

Skincare review

Retinoids, exfoliating acids, benzoyl peroxide, strong brightening products and recent procedures may affect timing. Bring accurate product information.

Patch testing and planning

A patch test is completed where required. Treatment timing, expected reactions, preparation and aftercare are then discussed before a full appointment is arranged.

Please be completely honest during consultation. Withholding medication, recent sun exposure or product use can increase the chance of an unwanted reaction.

What happens on treatment day?

Your appointment follows a structured process so the skin is assessed and prepared before laser treatment begins.

Arrival and skin check

Your skin is reviewed for any changes since consultation, including irritation, sun exposure, new medication or active breakouts.

Cleanse and preparation

Makeup, oil and surface residue are removed. Eye protection is used and the treatment area is prepared.

Carbon application

A thin, even layer of carbon lotion is applied and allowed to settle on the skin.

Controlled laser passes

The laser is used in planned stages according to your skin response and treatment protocol. You may hear light clicks and feel warmth or snapping sensations.

Final skin assessment

The carbon is removed and the skin is checked for the expected immediate response.

Aftercare guidance

You receive clear instructions about cleansing, hydration, active products, heat, exercise, sun protection and when to ask for advice.

Expected results and treatment course

Immediately after treatment, the skin may look cleaner and brighter, although temporary redness or warmth can occur. Over the following days, some clients notice that skin feels smoother and looks more refreshed.

A single treatment can be selected as an occasional refresh. A course of three may suit clients seeking a more structured plan. Maintenance depends on skin response, lifestyle, skincare and goals.

Results are individual. No outcome, pore-size change, acne improvement or duration of result can be guaranteed.

Downtime and possible reactions

Downtime is normally limited, but “minimal downtime” does not mean that every person will have no visible reaction.

  • Temporary redness or warmth
  • Mild tightness, dryness or sensitivity
  • Temporary flaking or roughness
  • Increased sensitivity to active products
  • Less commonly, prolonged irritation or pigment change

Contact the clinic if you experience an unexpected or worsening reaction.

Carbon Facial aftercare

Good aftercare protects the skin barrier and supports a comfortable recovery.

Keep skincare gentle

Use a mild cleanser and simple moisturiser as advised. Avoid scrubs, cleansing brushes and aggressive exfoliation while the skin is sensitive.

Pause strong actives

Do not immediately restart retinoids, AHA/BHA acids, benzoyl peroxide or strong brightening products. Follow the timing provided for your skin.

Reduce heat and friction

Avoid very hot showers, steam rooms, saunas, swimming and intense exercise initially. Do not rub, pick or scratch the treated skin.

Use sun protection

Protect treated skin from direct sun and use suitable broad-spectrum SPF. Sun exposure can increase sensitivity and pigmentation risk.

Makeup and products

Leave the skin clean where possible. Reintroduce makeup only when advised and use clean applicators to reduce irritation.

Carbon Laser Facial Oxford FAQ

What is a Carbon Laser Facial?

A Carbon Laser Facial is a non-invasive laser skin treatment in which a fine carbon lotion is applied to cleansed skin before controlled laser passes. It is also commonly called a Carbon Peel or Hollywood Peel.

Does a Carbon Facial hurt?

Most clients describe warmth, tingling or a light snapping sensation. Comfort varies, and the treatment settings are selected according to the consultation, patch test and skin response.

How many treatments will I need?

A single treatment may be chosen as a refresh. A course of three may suit clients who want a planned series. The best approach depends on skin condition, goals, response and budget.

Is there downtime?

Downtime is generally minimal, but temporary redness, warmth, tightness, dryness or sensitivity can occur. Individual reactions vary.

Do I need a consultation and patch test?

Yes. BARE by Marlese uses a consultation-led process and completes patch testing where required before a full treatment proceeds.

Should I stop retinol or exfoliating acids?

You may be asked to pause retinoids, exfoliating acids, benzoyl peroxide or other strong active products. Exact timing depends on the product, your skin and practitioner guidance.

Is the treatment available to Oxford students?

Adults can book subject to consultation, identification and suitability. Study schedules, current medication and active skincare should be discussed honestly during consultation.

Can university or hospital staff book evening appointments?

Evening appointments are displayed during weekday clinic hours, subject to availability. This may suit clients working or studying in Oxford during the day.

Is a Carbon Facial suitable for pigmentation?

Pigmentation has different causes and requires careful assessment. A Carbon Facial should not be presented as a guaranteed pigmentation treatment, and some concerns may require medical or specialist advice.

Can I have treatment before graduation or a conference?

Potentially, but do not leave your first session until immediately before the event. A consultation and patch test are required, and enough time should be allowed for any temporary sensitivity to settle.

Is this the same as a chemical peel?

No. A Carbon Laser Facial uses carbon lotion and controlled laser passes. A chemical peel uses acids or other chemical agents to exfoliate the skin. Suitability, expected effects and aftercare differ.

Why travel outside Oxford for a facial?

Some clients prefer easier access, a quieter setting and a one-to-one appointment. The best choice depends on your priorities, suitability and willingness to travel.
