Important Treatment Information

Laser tattoo removal uses laser energy to break tattoo pigment into smaller particles so the body can gradually clear them. Results vary from person to person. Multiple sessions are usually required and complete removal cannot be guaranteed.
  • I understand that the number of sessions required depends on factors including tattoo age, ink depth, ink colour, density, location, previous tattoo work, immune response, skin type and aftercare.
  • I understand that black and dark inks usually respond better than some colours, and that green, blue, yellow, white, pastel, cosmetic and mixed inks may be more resistant or unpredictable.
  • I understand that treatment may be uncomfortable and may feel hot, sharp, stinging or snapping during the session.
  • I understand that a patch test may be required and that the treated area must be monitored before full treatment proceeds.
  • I understand I must avoid active tanning, sunbeds and fake tan before treatment, and I must follow the aftercare instructions given.

Risks & Possible Side Effects

Please read carefully. Some effects are expected and temporary; others are uncommon but possible. By signing, you confirm these risks have been explained and you accept them.
