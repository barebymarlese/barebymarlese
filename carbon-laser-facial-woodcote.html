Every Carbon Laser Facial for clients from Woodcote begins with a personalised consultation. Your skin, medical history, current products and treatment goals are reviewed to confirm suitability.

The skin is cleansed and a fine layer of carbon lotion is applied. The carbon settles across the skin before controlled laser passes are carried out.

AFTERCARE

Simple aftercare helps support your skin following a Carbon Laser Facial and reduces unnecessary sensitivity.

Immediately After Treatment

Skin may feel warm or appear mildly pink. This usually settles quickly. Keep the skin clean and avoid touching unnecessarily.

First 24–48 Hours

Use gentle skincare, moisturiser and SPF. Avoid strong actives, retinoids, exfoliating acids and aggressive exfoliation.

Avoid

Avoid excessive heat, saunas, steam rooms, tanning and prolonged direct sun exposure as advised after treatment.

Ongoing Skin Care

Follow your personalised skincare guidance and use daily sun protection to support your treatment results.

FAQ

What is a Carbon Laser Facial?

A Carbon Laser Facial uses a fine carbon lotion and controlled laser passes to cleanse the skin and support a brighter, refreshed-looking complexion.

Does a Carbon Facial hurt?

Most clients describe mild warmth or a light snapping sensation. The treatment is generally considered comfortable.

Is there downtime?

Downtime is minimal for most clients. Mild warmth, pinkness or sensitivity may occur temporarily.

How many treatments will I need?

Some clients choose a single facial, while others select a course of three depending on their skin goals and suitability.

Do I need a patch test?

A consultation and patch test may be required depending on your skin, medical history, products and previous reactions.

What should I avoid beforehand?

Avoid tanning and follow guidance about retinoids, exfoliating acids and other strong skincare products before treatment.

Can I wear makeup afterwards?

It is usually best to keep the skin clean and avoid heavy makeup immediately after treatment. Follow the aftercare advice given.

When will I see results?

Skin may look cleaner and refreshed soon after treatment, although individual results vary and a course may be recommended.

Where is treatment carried out?

Treatment for clients from Woodcote is carried out at the private BARE by Marlese clinic in Woodcote, South Oxfordshire.
