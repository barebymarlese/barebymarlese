Laser Treatments Treatment Policies & Booking Information

All treatments at BARE by Marlese are tailored to your individual needs following a consultation and patch test.

Laser Tattoo Removal treatment plans are based on factors such as tattoo size, colour, age, depth, location, skin type and your body's natural ability to remove fragmented ink. Whether your goal is complete removal or fading for a cover-up, every treatment plan is personalised. Complete tattoo removal cannot be guaranteed, and the number of sessions required varies from person to person.

Carbon Laser Facial treatments are designed to improve overall skin health and appearance by targeting excess oil, enlarged pores, congestion, uneven skin tone and fine lines while stimulating collagen production. Visible improvements are often seen after the first treatment, with the best results typically achieved through a course of treatments combined with good skincare, sun protection and following the recommended treatment intervals. Individual results will vary depending on skin type, skin condition and lifestyle factors.

Fungal Toenail Laser Treatment works by targeting the fungal infection within the nail while allowing healthy nail growth over time. As nails grow slowly, visible improvement takes several months, and multiple treatments may be recommended depending on the severity of the infection. Good foot hygiene and following aftercare advice are essential to reduce the risk of reinfection.

Treatment intervals are recommended to maximise both safety and results. Laser tattoo removal treatments require a minimum interval of 6 weeks between sessions to allow the skin to heal and the body to naturally remove fragmented ink. Carbon Laser Facial and Fungal Toenail Laser treatments are normally scheduled at intervals recommended during your consultation based on your individual treatment plan.

Treatment bundles must be paid in full at the time of purchase and are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase unless otherwise agreed in writing. Any unused sessions remaining after this period will expire and cannot be extended.

Consultation and patch test deposits are non-refundable but may be transferred to another appointment if at least 24 hours' notice is provided.

A minimum of 24 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule any appointment. Late cancellations, missed appointments or failure to attend without notice may result in the treatment session being deducted from your bundle or forfeited where applicable.

AFTERCARE

Following Carbon Laser Facial aftercare helps calm the skin, maintain hydration and support the best possible result.

VIEW FULL AFTERCARE GUIDE

Immediately After Treatment

The skin may feel warm and can look mildly pink or flushed. This normally settles quickly. Keep the skin clean and avoid touching it unnecessarily.

First 48 Hours

Use gentle, fragrance-free skincare and a simple moisturiser. Avoid scrubs, exfoliating acids, retinoids and other strong active products until the skin feels settled.

Avoid

Avoid tanning, direct sun exposure, hot baths, saunas, steam rooms and intense exercise for the period advised after treatment.

Ongoing Skin Care

Use daily broad-spectrum SPF and follow the personalised skincare and treatment interval advice discussed during your consultation.

FAQ

What is a Carbon Laser Facial?

A Carbon Laser Facial uses a layer of carbon lotion followed by controlled laser passes to cleanse the skin's surface and improve the appearance of pores, oiliness and texture.

Does a Carbon Facial hurt?

Most clients describe mild warmth or a light snapping sensation. Comfort and skin response are monitored throughout the treatment.

Is there downtime?

Downtime is usually minimal, although temporary warmth, mild redness or sensitivity can occur. Individual skin responses vary.

How many treatments will I need?

Some clients choose a single facial for a refreshed appearance, while others select a course of three. Your recommended plan depends on your skin and goals.

Can I wear makeup afterwards?

It is generally best to keep the skin clean immediately after treatment and follow the personalised advice provided at your appointment.

What products should I avoid beforehand?

Strong retinoids, exfoliating acids, irritating actives and tanning should be avoided for the period advised before treatment.

Who may not be suitable?

Suitability depends on medical history, current medication, active skin conditions, recent tanning and the products being used. This is assessed during consultation.

When will I see a difference?

The skin can look fresher soon after treatment, while a course may provide more gradual improvement. Results vary between clients.

Is a patch test required?

A consultation and patch test are completed where required before treatment, allowing skin response and suitability to be assessed.
