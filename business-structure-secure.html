Business Summary

Core Structure BARE by Marlese runs through a website-led client flow. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, performance and workers are handled through Cloudflare, consultations are submitted via Cloudflare Worker, emails are handled through Gmail and Resend, files are stored in Google Drive, deposits are taken through Revolut Pro, and appointments are completed through Google Calendar Appointment Scheduling.
GoDaddy GitHub Pages Cloudflare Resend Gmail Google Drive Google Calendar Revolut Pro

Main Objective

Client Journey Goal

Keep the client journey simple and premium: complete the consultation first, receive confirmation, move to the deposit page, pay the £30 deposit through Revolut Pro, return to the website tab, unlock booking, and then complete the appointment booking through Google Calendar.

Internal Compliance & Incident Logging

Purpose

  • Used to record accidents, incidents, near misses, adverse reactions, and safety-related events
  • Supports practitioner record keeping and internal compliance
  • Provides a clear written record for insurance, follow-up, and business safety documentation

System Structure

  • Form hosted on GitHub Pages
  • Submission handled through Cloudflare Worker
  • Email delivery handled through Resend and Gmail
  • Records can be retained in Gmail, downloaded as PDF, and stored in Google Drive

How It Is Used

  • Completed by the practitioner or responsible person
  • Not part of the normal client booking journey
  • Used only when an incident, accident, or adverse event needs to be formally recorded
  • Designed to fit the same low-risk, serverless business structure as the rest of the website
Where this fits
The incident log sits inside the internal business compliance structure rather than the public client journey. It supports safe record keeping without adding a database, login area, or extra backend risk to the main website.
