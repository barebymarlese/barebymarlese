Privacy Policy

BARE by Marlese is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data Controller

BARE by Marlese
Email: marlese@barebymarlese.com
Provider of laser tattoo removal, carbon facial and fungal toenail laser treatments.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following information:

  • Personal details such as your name, email address and phone number
  • Your selected treatment, appointment date, appointment time and booking status
  • Information relating to cancellations, rescheduling and completed treatment sessions
  • Fitzpatrick skin type and skin classification information
  • Medical contraindications and relevant health screening information
  • Medication information
  • Consultation forms, consent records, patch test records, treatment notes and aftercare records
  • Information about laser tattoo removal, carbon laser facial or fungal toenail laser treatments
  • Photographs of treatment areas taken before, during or after treatment for clinical, progress-monitoring and insurance purposes
  • Payment status, amount paid, discount or promotional code information and payment transaction references
  • Technical information submitted through our website, such as form submission records and security-verification information

3. Special Category Data

Some information collected, including medical details and skin type, is classified as special category data under UK GDPR. This information is collected solely for the purpose of ensuring safe and appropriate treatment.

4. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Providing consultations, patch tests and treatments
  • Assessing treatment suitability and client safety
  • Managing appointments, treatment courses and session bookings
  • Processing appointment cancellations and rescheduling requests
  • Sending booking confirmations, appointment reminders and service-related notifications
  • Sending treatment, aftercare and follow-up information where appropriate
  • Processing deposits, treatment payments, refunds and payment-status updates
  • Maintaining consultation, patch test, booking, payment and treatment records
  • Monitoring treatment progress and outcomes
  • Preventing duplicate bookings, payment errors, fraud and misuse of our systems
  • Meeting insurance, legal, regulatory and record-keeping requirements

5. Legal Basis

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent, contractual necessity where treatments are booked, and legal obligations relating to health and safety and record keeping.

6. Data Storage & Security

Personal data is processed through secure systems used and operated by BARE by Marlese. These include our website forms, custom-built booking and administration systems, appointment database, treatment records and notification systems.

Our booking system may store information including your contact details, selected treatment, appointment date and time, booking status, treatment-session history, cancellation or rescheduling information, payment status and transaction references.

Website and booking services may be hosted using trusted technology and infrastructure providers. Data may therefore be processed by providers that support website hosting, database storage, security, email delivery and payment processing.

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information against accidental loss, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. Access is limited to authorised persons and service providers where required to operate the clinic and its systems.

7. Data Retention

Consultation forms, patch test records, treatment records and clinical photographs may be retained for up to 7 years in accordance with insurance and legal requirements for aesthetic and laser treatment providers.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your data, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion where legally permitted, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact: marlese@barebymarlese.com

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

10. Online Booking & Client Notifications

Online appointments are managed through a custom-built booking and administration system operated by BARE by Marlese.

When you make or manage a booking, we may use your name, email address, phone number, selected treatment, appointment details, booking status and treatment-session information to manage your consultation, patch test or treatment.

This information may be used to send essential service communications, including:

  • Consultation and appointment confirmations
  • Payment confirmations and payment-status notifications
  • Appointment reminders
  • Cancellation and rescheduling confirmations
  • Treatment-session booking updates
  • Aftercare and treatment follow-up information

These communications are not marketing messages. They are sent where necessary to administer your booking, treatment and ongoing care.

11. Payments & Stripe

Deposits and treatment payments for consultations, patch tests, laser tattoo removal, carbon laser facial treatments and fungal toenail laser treatments may be processed securely by Stripe.

When you make a payment, Stripe may process information such as your name, email address, payment amount, payment method, discount or promotional code information and transaction details in order to complete and verify the payment.

BARE by Marlese does not store or have access to your complete payment-card or banking details. We may receive and retain limited payment information from Stripe, including the amount paid, payment status, payment date, refund status and transaction reference.

Payment information may be linked to your booking so that deposits, balances, refunds and treatment payments can be recorded correctly within our booking and administration systems.

Stripe processes personal information in accordance with its own privacy policy and legal obligations.
