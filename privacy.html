Privacy Policy

1. Data Controller

BARE by Marlese
Email: marlese@barebymarlese.com

2. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Medical and skin-related consultation data
  • Treatment history and notes
  • Uploaded images and consultation photos
  • Appointment and booking information

3. Special Category Data

Medical and skin-related information is treated as special category data under UK GDPR and is collected solely to ensure safe treatment.

4. How Your Data Is Used

  • To assess treatment suitability
  • To manage bookings and appointments
  • To communicate with you regarding your treatment
  • To maintain professional treatment records

5. Legal Basis

Processing is based on:

  • Your explicit consent
  • Contractual necessity (bookings/treatments)
  • Legal obligations (health and safety)

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely processed using Cloudflare infrastructure, including secure Workers and database storage.

Access is restricted and appropriate security measures are in place to prevent unauthorised access.

7. Data Retention

Consultation and treatment records may be stored for up to 7 years in line with insurance and legal requirements.

8. Payments

Payments may be processed through secure third-party providers such as Stripe.

BARE by Marlese does not store full payment details.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Contact: marlese@barebymarlese.com

10. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office if you believe your data is mishandled.

11. Important
