Policy Statement

Core Hygiene Standards

Treatment Room Controls

  • Only essential treatment items are present in the working area.
  • Clean and dirty items are kept separate.
  • Food and drink are excluded from the treatment area.
  • Surfaces are maintained in a cleanable condition and checked routinely.
  • Ventilation is maintained as appropriate to the treatment and products used.
