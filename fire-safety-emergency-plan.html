Fire Safety & Emergency Plan

Home-based fire safety and emergency plan for laser tattoo removal treatments carried out at BARE by Marlese. This document is designed to support safe day-to-day operation, emergency response, council inspection readiness and insurer expectations for a small treatment room environment.

This plan is intended to support compliance with fire safety duties for business premises by identifying fire hazards, persons at risk, control measures, evacuation arrangements, equipment checks and emergency actions. It should be read alongside the business fire risk assessment, equipment maintenance records, COSHH documentation and laser local rules.
