FAQ

Can I remove a tattoo completely?

Some tattoos can fade significantly or be removed, but complete removal cannot be guaranteed. Ink colour, depth, age and skin type all matter.

How many sessions will I need?

Most tattoos require multiple sessions. A realistic estimate can be discussed at consultation after assessing the tattoo.

Can I fade a tattoo for a cover-up?

Yes. Many clients choose laser tattoo fading to make a cover-up easier and cleaner for their tattoo artist.
